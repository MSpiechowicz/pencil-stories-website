Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how the PencilStories publishing tool (“the app”, “we”) handles data. The app is a private, internal tool. It is used only by the creator of the PencilStories channel to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no public users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube API Services.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: after the channel owner signs in with Google, the app uses the scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos and thumbnails) and youtube.readonly (to read the channel's own uploads and confirm that a video was published).
  • Instagram: the app uses the Instagram Graph API with the owner's own business account to publish Reels and read their publishing status.

The app does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other users. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the creator's own videos (video file, title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy setting and schedule) and to check that publishing succeeded. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer and are never shared.
  • The app keeps a local log of what it published (video IDs and URLs) so the same video is not uploaded twice.
  • For Instagram, the creator's own video is uploaded for a short time to cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) at an unlisted URL so Instagram can fetch it, and it is deleted once Instagram has published it.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram), and only to publish the creator's own content on the creator's own accounts.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the Google security settings.
  • Instagram access can be removed in Instagram or Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
  • To delete everything the app stores, the creator deletes the local token files and the publishing log. To ask about deletion, use the contact link below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone other than the channel owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
