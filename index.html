What is PencilStories?

PencilStories is a channel of short, animated motivational stories. Every story is drawn line by line on notebook paper, with a pencil, stick-figure characters and a calm narrator, about ordinary people getting through hard moments.

New stories are published on YouTube (full videos and Shorts) and on Instagram (Reels).

The PencilStories publishing tool

The PencilStories app is a private, internal tool used only by the channel's creator. It renders the finished videos and uploads them to the creator's own PencilStories YouTube channel and Instagram account, using the official YouTube Data API and Instagram Graph API.

  • It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.
  • It only uploads videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails that the creator made.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or other YouTube or Instagram users.

Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
